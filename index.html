I am currently a Professor with the School of Computer and Artificial Intelligence, Beijing Technology and Business University (BTBU). I received my Ph.D. degree in Computer Science from Nanyang Technological University (NTU) in 2014, my M.S. degree in Applied Mathematics from Zhejiang University in 2009, and my B.S. degree in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Zhejiang University in 2007.

My research interests include Intelligent Geometry Processing, Computer Graphics, Image Processing, 3D Reconstruction, and Deep Learning.
